How to Buy

Ways to bid

In the saleroom

Register at the front desk on sale day and receive a paddle.

By telephone

A staff member calls you during the sale to bid on your behalf.

Online

Bid live from anywhere through our online bidding platform.

Absentee

Leave a maximum bid and we will bid for you as low as possible.

Buyer's premium

A premium of 25% is added to the hammer price of each lot.

Payment & collection

Payment is due within 7 days. We can recommend shippers worldwide.
